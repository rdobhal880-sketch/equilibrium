        How Equilibrium works

        The problem: exam season pushes students to trade sleep and movement for revision hours, and stress quietly builds. Most wellbeing apps track one thing. Equilibrium tracks the balance between three, because they affect each other.

        Who it's for

        Secondary students (IB, A-level, GCSE, CBSE) in the UAE, especially in the months before mocks and finals. It works on a phone in under a minute and needs no account.

        How the Balance score is calculated

        Rest 100 when sleep is 8–10 h. Minus 25 points for every hour under 8, minus 15 for every hour over 10.

        Body 80% from active minutes (60 min = full marks, WHO target) + 20% from water against your daily goal.

        Mind 60% from mood (1–5) + 40% from how calm you feel (stress 1–10, inverted).

        Balance = average of the three − 20% of the gap between your strongest and weakest pillar.

        The gap penalty is the "equilibrium" idea, borrowed from economics: a system is stable when the forces are even, not when one is huge. Sleeping 10 hours doesn't cancel out zero movement.

        Safety and accuracy

        • Every health number on the site is taken from a named source (listed below) and checked against it.
        • If a check-in shows very low mood with very high stress, or a thought record mentions self-harm, the app gently shows UAE support lines. It never diagnoses.
        • The "Need support?" button is on every screen.
        • Insights are described as patterns, not causes, and the app says so.
        • No data leaves the device. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics.

        How it was built

        Equilibrium was built with AI-assisted ("vibe") coding, then reviewed, tested and changed by hand. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks, so every line can be read and edited.
